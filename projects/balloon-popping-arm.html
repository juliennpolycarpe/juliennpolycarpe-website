Balloon-Popping Robotic Arm

1st Place — Robotics Hackathon

A joystick-controlled robotic arm built for a robotics hackathon, where the challenge was to pop as many balloons as possible from a fixed distance. The arm chained multiple servos together to give it 3 degrees of freedom, controlled live via joystick, with a toothpick mounted at the tip as the popping mechanism. Positioned roughly 5 feet from a rack of 15 balloons, the arm popped every one — taking first place.

  • Chained multiple servo motors together to achieve 3 degrees of freedom, giving the arm enough reach and precision to hit each target
  • Mapped joystick input to real-time servo positioning for direct manual control
  • Mounted a toothpick as the end effector, precise enough to pop all 15 balloons in the rack

Arduino IDE · Servo Motors · Joystick Control

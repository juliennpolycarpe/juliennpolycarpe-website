Autonomous Wall-Following Car

A self-navigating car built on a breadboard, using ultrasonic distance sensors to track its distance from a wall on either side and steer accordingly, with a light sensor governing separate light-seeking behavior. LEDs provided status/headlight output.

Arduino IDE · Ultrasonic Distance Sensors · Photoresistors · Breadboard

Julien Polycarpe — Worcester, MA / Brooklyn, NY

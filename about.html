About

I'm a junior at WPI studying Electrical & Computer Engineering and Computer Science, splitting time between signal chains and software stacks. My coursework and projects sit at the boundary between the two — writing the firmware for a sensor and the interface that reads it, or the FSM that drives a traffic light and the ORM behind a web platform.

Dean's List, LSAMP Scholar. Based in Brooklyn, NY; on campus in Worcester, MA the rest of the year.

Outside of class I'm a Senator in WPI's Student Government Association, Treasurer of the Black Student Union, DEI Director and Judicial Board Member for WPI's Interfraternity Council, and Scholarship Chair for Theta Chi.
